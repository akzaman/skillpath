# National Education Center — User guide

Live site: https://skillpath-lac.vercel.app

This guide is for **students** and **teachers**. Switch the site language with **EN / IT / বাং** in the top bar. The same guide is also on the site at `/guide`.

---

## Student

A student account lets you enroll, watch lectures, keep notes, and pick up exactly where you stopped.

### 1. Create an account

1. Open [Log in](https://skillpath-lac.vercel.app/login).
2. Choose **Sign up**.
3. Enter your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
4. Submit. You are a student.

On the live site, sign-up is **email and password**. Google / X are not used there.

### 2. Choose a language

Use **EN / IT / বাং** in the header. Menus and buttons change immediately. The choice is saved in your browser.

### 3. Find a course

- **Explore** or **Courses** opens the catalog.
- Search CAF, A2, Patente B, dichiarazione, or another desk.
- Filter by category: tax, CAF, Italian language, driving licence, immigration, work.
- Open a card to read the syllabus, lectures, and instructor.

### 4. Enroll

On the course page:

- **Enroll now** / **Add to My learning** starts the course.
- **Wishlist** saves it without enrolling.
- Preview lectures play without an account. Full lectures need you to be signed in.

### 5. Watch a lecture

- Open a lecture from the course page or from **Continue** on the dashboard.
- The player saves your place about every four seconds, and again when you pause or finish.
- Space bar plays and pauses.
- Mark the lecture **complete**, or let the video end do it.

### 6. Take notes

On the watch page, write in the notes panel. Notes are stored on your account for that lecture.

### 7. Track progress

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Courses, finished lectures, time watched, Continue card, role switcher |
| **Progress** | Every enrolled course with a lecture checklist |
| **My learning** | Courses you enrolled in |
| **Wishlist** | Courses you saved |

Progress is tied to the signed-in account.

### 8. Switch role later

On **Dashboard**, use **Your access** to open Teacher or Admin on the same account. Switch back to Student to keep learning.

## Teacher

The studio is where you draft a course, add lectures, publish to the catalog, and see who enrolled.

### 1. Open teacher access

1. Sign in.
2. Go to **Dashboard**.
3. Under **Your access**, choose **Teacher**.

Or open **Teach** and apply. An admin can approve the application. Admins already have studio access.

### 2. Enter the studio

**Teach** in the header opens your list: drafts, published count, lectures, and students.

**New course** starts a draft. Nothing is public until you publish.

### 3. Create a course

Fill in:

- Title and short subtitle
- Description
- Desk (category) and level
- Poster
- Instructor name, title, and bio

Save. You go to the editor.

### 4. Add lectures

Open the **Lectures** tab.

- Title, summary, optional transcript
- A clip from the video library, a **video file upload** (MP4/WebM up to 4 MB), or a **custom URL** (hosted MP4, YouTube, or Vimeo)
- Poster: pick a default, **upload a photo**, or paste an image URL
- **Preview** on if guests may watch that lesson without enrolling

Add **at least one lecture** before you publish.

### 5. Edit and reorder

- Arrows change lecture order
- Edit a lesson to change the video or copy
- Delete a lesson you no longer need
- **Details** updates title, poster (upload or URL), and instructor
- **Students** lists everyone enrolled
- Admins can open any catalogue/demo course with **Edit content** (Teach or Admin → Catalog). That copies it into the studio so title, lectures, poster, and video can change.

### 6. Publish

- **Publish** puts the course in the public catalog.
- **Unpublish** hides it again. Enrollments and progress stay.
- After it is live, students open the course page and start watching.

### 7. Teach well

Keep lessons short — one form, one desk, one conversation. Write the summary as the thing a student can do after the last minute. Check the **Students** tab after you go live.
